Most companies often struggle to have organizational readiness early on when they ship. This creates massive problems down the line.

As more people join the company, and as the problems compound, you must ensure alignment. That is why you must have a centralized alignment & decision-making framework. You have to do this right from the start, because it get much harder to change later on.Your hires might be focused on juicing up the marketing metrics or the sales metrics, which might look good in isolation. However, they might not be necessarily driving actual progress for the company.
